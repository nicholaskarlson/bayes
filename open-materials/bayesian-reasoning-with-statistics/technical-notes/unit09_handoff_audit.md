# Unit 9 reproducible handoff audit (optional)

Unit 9 reuses the 36-row fictional convenience file from Unit 7, its dictionary, provenance, model specification, full-precision reference and independent calculations. It introduces **no new observations or estimator**. The report builder reads the unchanged CSV, checks unique IDs, route and outcome coding, counts and SHA-256 against the existing reference, derives simple Beta shape/mean and predictive-ratio arithmetic, then quotes the Unit 7 exact difference interval and tail probabilities from its previously independently audited reference. The builder does not reintegrate those difference tails or claim to be an independent numerical implementation.

From the repository root, `python3 open-materials/bayesian-reasoning-with-statistics/scripts/build_unit09_handoff.py` writes `review/unit09_case_report.md` and `review/unit09_case_evidence.json`. The directory is ignored by Git. The evidence JSON includes source digests, counts, full-precision selected values, a conditional scope statement and provenance of computation. The report is deterministic; no timestamp or claimed commit ID is invented. It is a teaching report, not a released publication or registered analysis plan.

The public `make verify` route checks raw counts, source hashes, analyzer output and generated Unit 9 evidence; `make r-audit` includes `verify_unit09_in_R.R`, which reads the raw file, independently integrates the positive-difference probability, computes both posterior means and uses `lbeta` ratios for zero-count predictions. The Unit 7 base-R verifier checks the full reference including the difference interval. The authoring suite also checks the chapter's exact wording, which is outside this code-only public companion.

The primary model assumes independent Bernoulli reports within route and independent Beta(1,1) priors, giving desk Beta(9,17) and poster Beta(1,13). Sensitivity replaces both priors with Beta(2,2), giving Beta(10,18) and Beta(2,14). The first difference interval is [0.04152285318893968, 0.48982544613971385]; the second is [−0.016721156270119813, 0.45636163145584224]. The posterior positive-direction probabilities are 0.987467306015693 and 0.9673916204583348. Priors predict zero of twelve poster reports with probabilities 1/13 and 1/35; posterior predictive probabilities for another comparable twelve are 0.52 and approximately 0.299. The difference probabilities and intervals are conditional on the named models and records. Do not infer posterior model probabilities or a route effect from their difference.

The 0.10 threshold is an unvalidated teaching magnitude. The raw gap is a file fraction, not a population estimate with a guaranteed sampling interpretation. Data/analysis checks cannot validate actual self-report, nonresponse, route selection or a campus frame. Prior/posterior predictive counts are in-sample model criticism, not held-out calibration or transport. No MCMC sampler was run; chain statistics are inapplicable. The generated report intentionally ends with a next-study plan rather than a recommendation to close a route.
