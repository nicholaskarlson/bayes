# Topic crosswalk: jamovi topics and this book

This crosswalk maps the 27 chapters of *Psychology Research Methods and Statistics by Design with jamovi* (J1–J27) to the units of *Bayesian Reasoning with Statistics* (U1–U9). The jamovi chapters are an inventory of what readers are likely to know already, **not** a promise to translate every test. “Core” means taught and checked in this book, “Context” means a short comparison or boundary, and “Further study” means intentionally outside this book. *Causal Reasoning* and *Predictive Reasoning* are other volumes in the series.

## Coverage boundary

This shorter book deliberately does not contain all Bayesian analogues of the 27 chapters. It teaches transferable ideas through representative cases. Python/R paths replace jamovi GUI actions rather than imitating menus. A posterior predictive check in U4/U8 examines model implications using fitted data; evaluation of held-out predictions, leakage and calibration belongs to *Predictive Reasoning*. None of the planned examples establishes a causal effect merely by using Bayes.
