# Unit 7 optional technical audit

The source is `data/unit07_help_seeking_routes.csv`: unique D001–D024 and P001–P012 records with respectively eight and zero seven-day self-reports. This is a fictional nonrandom route contrast. With separate Beta(a,b) priors and independent Bernoulli likelihoods, the two posteriors are `Beta(a+8,b+16)` and `Beta(a,b+12)`. For the primary (a,b)=(1,1) they are Beta(9,17) and Beta(1,13). For sensitivity (2,2) they are Beta(10,18) and Beta(2,14). There is no partial pooling between routes. A 2×2 odds ratio is intentionally not the model target.

The difference Δ=θ_D−θ_P has support [−1,1]. For threshold h, the exact posterior tail is `P(Δ>h)=∫₀¹ f_D(x) F_P(x−h) dx`, with the Beta CDF clamped to zero below 0 and one above 1. A deterministic one-dimensional integration computes the zero and ten-percentage-point tails; bisection on the complementary CDF yields the central 95% interval. This integration is not simulation or MCMC. The standard-library Python audit evaluates integer-shape Beta CDFs as finite binomial sums and integrates by composite Simpson's rule; SciPy and base-R paths use their separate beta functions and quadrature. `expected-results/unit07_anchors.csv` preserves full numerical precision. Displayed three-decimal values must not be used as high-precision audit anchors.

With a Beta(a,b) prior and twelve new Bernoulli observations, `P(Y=0)=B(a,b+12)/B(a,b)`. After zero of twelve, replace b with b+12 for another comparable twelve. Primary values are 1/13=0.0769231 and 13/25=0.52. Sensitivity values are 0.0285714 and 0.2991453. The primary zero is somewhat uncommon *under the prior predictive model*, prompting examination of prior expectation and measurement; a posterior predictive zero is relatively plausible *under the fitted model*. These are limited model implications, not held-out validation.

This analysis assumes stable within-route report probabilities and independent records. The volunteered routes may differ in need, prior contact, accessibility or reporting. A route association cannot identify a route intervention's effect. No sampling frame supports a campus rate. The threshold h=.10 is illustrative and has no established utility. A deeper predictive performance exercise belongs in *Predictive Reasoning*; a causal design belongs in the causal volume.
