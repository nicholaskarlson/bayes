# Unit 5 fictional survey provenance

`data/unit05_belonging_study.csv` is newly generated teaching data: 72 fictional identifiers B001–B072, one row per volunteer. `scripts/generate_unit05.py` deterministically regenerates the exact CSV with Python's standard-library PRNG and seed 20260905. The author-side generator contains simulation parameters; those are *not* inferential truths for the reader, nor can the artificial data validate a population claim. No records were imported from the jamovi, causal or public companion repositories. The 0–100 self-report scores and weekly-hours numbers are invented, not human responses. There are no missing cells. The survey neither assigns hours nor draws a probability sample. The unbounded normal likelihood is a deliberate working approximation to a bounded score and is challenged with a prior predictive range check.

The CSV/dictionary/model/expected record and scripts are separately covered by the resource SHA256 manifest. The independent verifier regenerates the CSV twice in temporary directories and compares bytes with the committed data; it then recomputes inference from the raw CSV without importing `analyze_unit05.py`.
